Bieber or Morbid Angel first?

by Mart Kawaii • August 22, 2012 • 0 Comments

Tonight, as I was chatting with my friend Kremena about serial killers and dentists, a very important question appeared in my mind. Only you can help me solve it.

Imagine that you must listen to Justin Bieber and Morbid Angel in a row. Would you prefer to listen to Justin Bieber first and then to Morbid Angel or to listen to Morbid Angel and then to Justin Bieber?

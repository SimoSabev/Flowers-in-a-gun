Interview with MNDR @ Brooklyn Night Bazaar

by Mart Kawaii • January 25, 2014 • 0 Comments

Amanda Warner, better known as MNDR – the face and the voice of the studio project with Peter Wade, that ruled 2013 with songs like “Feed me diamonds” and “Bang Bang Bang” feat. Mark Ronson – kicked ass at Brooklyn Night Bazaar with bouncy indietronica set.

We were there to film it, speak to Amanda right before the show (and dance in the mean time). Here is the result – 8 minutes of HD video, in which she speaks about her inspirations, the music she played as a DJ and her upcoming projects with Kylie Minogue, Sean Paul and “may be” Mark Ronson again.

Enjoy!

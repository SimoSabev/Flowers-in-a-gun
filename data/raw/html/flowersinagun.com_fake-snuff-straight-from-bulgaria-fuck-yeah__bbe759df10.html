Fake snuff straight from Bulgaria? Fuck yeah!

by Mart Kawaii • February 21, 2012 • 0 Comments

Today is officially the day of the ultimate laziness and random forgotten findings, as I’m laying the whole time with the laptop on my legs and a glass of wine in the one hand, the finger of the free one curiously roving through the keyboard and the folders and the net… That’s exactly how I got to the next two videos. They were shooted, directed and edited in Sofia,Bulgaria by my friend Steffen Von Fliesch, who’s a lowlife and a freak, but also a fucking genius!

Starring the beautiful Gloria as the bondaged girl (I forgot how much I love your ass in nylons, bitch…) and Zaikata (The Rabbit) as the killer. Great job all!

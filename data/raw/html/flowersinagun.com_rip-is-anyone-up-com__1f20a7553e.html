IsAnyone Up.com is dead. Long live #NBHNC.

by Mart Kawaii • April 24, 2012 • 0 Comments

Oh well, I just found out pretty sad news. Randomly as usual, of course. Talking on Skype with my friend N. I told him about the absolut cult website Is Anyone Up.com on which everyone could post anonymously naked pictures of someone, hooked up to his Facebook. And they were beautiful tattoed kids, there, with big cocks or nice tits and pussies. Hipster Depravity Heaven in few words. It was also used widely to expose ex-girlfriends, famous in the scene guys you’ve fucked etc. I guess I won’t be that excited if I was exposed there. But hopefully I wasn’t. Ha-ha-ha!

Ok, honestly, on this website, 24/7 I could find one of my favourite things on world: hard big musician cocks. You can hardly beat that.

However, as I found through Wikipedia Is Anyone Up? is dead. It got shut down 5 days ago. April 19, 2012.

When you browse now IsAnyoneUp.com you can find an open letter from the creator Hunter Moore (THIS filthy bastard  not the cat though) and he’s gonna tell you more and better about it than me.

As a huge Cyber & Erotica,& CyberErotica fan and a writer involved in all this stuff I have to say.

I WILL MISS ANOTHER LEGEND.

Thanx Hunter. I must say. That. Was. Fun. And as its very very important to know when to leave, regretful, I have to say that probably you made the right move. Again.

Also some good news. I’m pretty sure that IsAnyOneup’s twitter will never die. Pure evil. 

Definitely there will be much less naked musicians (&groupies) online now. But I hope there will be more in real live. ]:)

Also… Very Important.

This party will be CULT. I will be working, but if you have the possibility to go – fucking do it.
